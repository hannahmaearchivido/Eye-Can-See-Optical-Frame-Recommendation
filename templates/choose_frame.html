Instructions

Follow these steps to capture your best suited frame:

1

Choose any eyeglass frames filtered by the system based on your condition and lens type.

2

Wear a different eyeglass frame per photo.

3

Position your face in front of the camera.

4

Click the "Start Capture" button.

5

Repeat the steps for the other frames. You can click the "Stop Capture" button if you are now satisfied with the frames you wore and tried.

The recommendation is based on the filtered frames that match the patient’s lens type and condition, how well each frame physically fits your face using the three primary parametric measures (Lens Width, Bridge Width, and Temple Length), and the results of the face shape analysis.
